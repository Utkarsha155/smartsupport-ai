# Warranty and Servicing

## Manufacturer warranty
Every watch comes with the official manufacturer's warranty, valid across India. The length depends on the brand:
- Omega: 5 years
- TAG Heuer and Longines: 2 years
- Tissot, Seiko and Citizen: 2 years
- Titan, Fossil and Casio: 1 to 2 years depending on the model
- Smartwatches (Apple, Samsung, Garmin): 1 year

## Chronos Care extended warranty
Customers can buy Chronos Care at checkout to extend the warranty by 1 extra year. It costs 5% of the watch price, with a minimum of ₹499. Chronos Care covers movement defects and crown or pusher failures. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage caused by misuse, scratches, or loss and theft.

## What the warranty does not cover
- Physical damage such as broken glass, dents or deep scratches.
- Damage caused by opening or repairing the watch at an unauthorised service centre.
- Normal wear of straps, batteries and the finish of the case.

## Servicing
- We offer free battery replacement for quartz watches bought from us, for life.
- Mechanical and automatic watches should be serviced every 3 to 5 years. We arrange servicing through the brand's authorised service centre.
- To book a service, email support@thechronoscollective.com with your order number, or visit our Pune experience centre.
- A standard service usually takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on the brand.
