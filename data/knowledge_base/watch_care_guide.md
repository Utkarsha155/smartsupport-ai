# Watch Care Guide and Product Help

## Water resistance explained
- 30m (3 ATM): splash resistant only, for example rain or washing hands. Do not swim with it.
- 50m (5 ATM): suitable for short swims in a pool, but not for diving.
- 100m (10 ATM): suitable for swimming and snorkelling.
- 200m (20 ATM) and above: suitable for diving.
Never press the buttons or pull out the crown while the watch is under water.

## Daily care
- Clean the case and metal bracelet with a soft, slightly damp microfiber cloth.
- Keep leather straps away from water, perfume and sweat to make them last longer.
- Keep watches away from strong magnets such as speakers and phone cases with magnetic clasps.

## Automatic watches
An automatic watch winds itself using the movement of your wrist. If you do not wear it for 1 to 2 days it may stop. To start it again, wind the crown gently 20 to 30 times and then set the time. A watch winder can keep it running when you are not wearing it.
