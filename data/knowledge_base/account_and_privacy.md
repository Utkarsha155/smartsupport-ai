# Account and Privacy

## Creating an account
Customers can sign up with an email address or a mobile number. Guest checkout is also available, but an account is needed to track orders easily, earn Chronos Points and save addresses.

## Forgot password
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email or mobile number. A reset link (for email) or an OTP (for mobile) is sent within a minute. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes.

## Updating account details
Name, phone number, saved addresses and communication preferences can be updated from "My Account" > "Profile". To change the registered email address, contact support for verification.

## Deleting an account
To permanently delete your account, email support@thechronoscollective.com from your registered email address. The account is deleted within 7 working days. Any unused Chronos Points are lost, but Chronos Credits can be refunded to your bank account on request before deletion.

## Privacy
We never sell customer data. Card details are processed by our PCI-DSS compliant payment partner and are never stored on our servers. Customers can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link at the bottom of every email.
