# Shipping and Delivery

## Delivery charges
- Free standard shipping on all orders above ₹2,000 within India.
- Orders below ₹2,000 have a flat shipping fee of ₹99.
- Express delivery is available for ₹249 in major metro cities (Mumbai, Pune, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata).

## Delivery timelines
- Standard delivery: 3 to 7 working days.
- Express delivery: 1 to 2 working days.
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on a working day are dispatched the same day.
- Remote areas and North-East states may take up to 10 working days.

## International shipping
We ship to more than 25 countries including the USA, UK, UAE, Singapore and Australia. International delivery takes 7 to 14 working days. Shipping is charged at checkout based on the destination. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer.

## Insured and secure packaging
All watches above ₹25,000 are shipped fully insured, in tamper-proof packaging, and need an OTP at the time of delivery. The customer must share the OTP only after checking that the outer package is sealed and not damaged.

## Store pickup
Customers in Pune can choose "Pick up from store" at checkout and collect the order from our Viman Nagar experience centre, usually within 24 hours. Pickup is free.
