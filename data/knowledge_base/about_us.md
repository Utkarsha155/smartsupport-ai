# About The Chronos Collective

The Chronos Collective is an online store for authentic luxury and everyday watches, based in Pune, India. We were founded in 2019 and ship across India and to 25+ countries.

## What we sell
- Luxury watches from brands such as Omega, TAG Heuer, Longines, Tissot and Seiko Presage.
- Everyday and sports watches from Casio G-Shock, Fossil, Titan and Citizen.
- Smartwatches from Apple, Samsung and Garmin.
- Accessories: leather and metal straps, watch winders, travel cases and cleaning kits.

## Authenticity promise
Every watch we sell is 100% genuine and sourced directly from the brand or an authorised distributor. Each watch ships with the original box, the manufacturer's warranty card and a Chronos Collective certificate of authenticity. If any product is ever found to be non-genuine, we refund 200% of the price paid.
