# Returns, Exchanges and Refunds

## Return window
Most products can be returned within 15 days of delivery. Smartwatches can be returned within 7 days of delivery.

## Conditions for a return
- The watch must be unworn, with no scratches or signs of use.
- The protective stickers and plastic films must still be in place.
- The original box, manuals, warranty card, tags and certificate of authenticity must be included.
- Watches that have been resized (links removed from a metal bracelet) can still be returned if the removed links are sent back.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Engraved or personalised watches.
- Watches bought during the Final Clearance Sale.
- Straps and cleaning kits once the seal is opened, for hygiene reasons.

## How to request a return
Go to "My Orders", choose the item and click "Return or Exchange". We arrange a free pickup within 2 working days. Our team inspects the watch within 3 working days of receiving it.

## Refunds
- Once the return passes inspection, the refund is started within 24 hours.
- Card and UPI refunds reach your account in 5 to 7 working days.
- Cash on Delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer (NEFT) in 5 to 7 working days, or instantly as Chronos Credits if you prefer.
- Chronos Credits never expire and can be used on any future order.

## Exchanges
You can exchange a watch for a different model or strap size within the same 15-day window. If the new watch costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, the difference is refunded as Chronos Credits.
