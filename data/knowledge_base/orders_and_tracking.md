# Orders and Order Tracking

## How to track an order
After dispatch, we send a tracking link by email and SMS. You can also track any order by logging in to your account and opening "My Orders". Order numbers start with "TCC-" followed by 8 digits, for example TCC-20481937.

## Order status meanings
- Confirmed: payment received and the order is being prepared.
- Quality Check: our team is checking the watch, box and papers before packing.
- Dispatched: handed over to the courier partner (Blue Dart or Delhivery).
- Out for Delivery: the courier will deliver it today.
- Delivered: the package has been delivered.

## Changing or cancelling an order
- An order can be cancelled for free any time before it is dispatched, from "My Orders" > "Cancel Order".
- After dispatch, the order cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery under our return policy.
- The delivery address can be changed only before dispatch, by contacting support.
- Refunds for cancelled orders are processed to the original payment method within 3 to 5 working days.

## Damaged or wrong item received
If the package is damaged or you received the wrong item, please record an unboxing video and contact support within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a free pickup and send a replacement or give a full refund.
