# Payments, EMI and Offers

## Accepted payment methods
- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express)
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and others)
- Net banking from all major Indian banks
- Cash on Delivery (COD), only for orders up to ₹50,000
- Chronos Credits (store credit)

International orders can be paid with international cards or PayPal.

## EMI options
- No-cost EMI for 3 and 6 months on orders above ₹10,000 with HDFC, ICICI, SBI and Axis Bank credit cards.
- Standard EMI for 9, 12 and 18 months is available on most credit cards, with interest charged by the bank.

## Current offers
- First order: 10% off up to ₹2,000 with code CHRONOS10.
- Festive offer: extra 5% off on all luxury watches above ₹50,000 with code TIMELESS5. Valid until 31 December 2026.
- Only one coupon code can be used per order.

## Chronos Rewards loyalty program
Customers earn 1 Chronos Point for every ₹100 spent. 100 points can be redeemed for ₹100 off on a future order. Points are added 15 days after delivery, once the return window is over.

## Invoices and GST
A GST invoice is emailed when the order is dispatched and can also be downloaded from "My Orders". To add a business GSTIN to the invoice, enter it at checkout before placing the order.
